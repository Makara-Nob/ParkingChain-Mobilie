## 2️⃣ File Name Rules

- ✅ **Correct:** `aba_logo.png` (lowercase, underscore)
- ❌ **Wrong:** `aba-logo.png` (no hyphens allowed)
- ❌ **Wrong:** `AbaLogo.png` (no uppercase allowed)
